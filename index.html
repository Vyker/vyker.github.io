03

The Approach — diagnose precisely. treat minimally. recover fully.

01

Diagnose precisely

A working diagnosis before any intervention — clinical exam first, then targeted imaging (ultrasound or MRI) only when it will change the plan.

02

Treat minimally

Conservative, exercise-led care first. Ultrasound-guided regenerative procedures when the body can repair itself — interventions that are minimal, image-checked, and proposed only when they change the outcome. Never a default, never a reflex to surgery.

03

Recover fully

Structured exercise prescription — movement medicine, healthy living. Measurable return-to-baseline targets, with a plan you can feel in your joints, not just your head.

04

For athletes

Football, running, tennis, and court and contact sports. The assessment that matters for sport is not just the injury — it is what the athlete can do, and when they can do it safely again.
