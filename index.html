About the consultant

Dr. Shimas Salih is a UK-trained Consultant in Physical Medicine & Rehabilitation with an MSK and sports-medicine focus. He studied at the University of Anbar, completed specialist training (CCT–PRM) at the North West Deanery in the UK, and has been in consultant practice for over 15 years.

In the UAE since 2022 — Healthpoint (Mubadala Health), Abu Dhabi, then DISC Sports Medicine in Dubai Healthcare City — he works from a simple rule: find out what is actually wrong first, do the least that is enough, and rebuild strength in a way you can measure.

03

The three-step approach

01

Diagnose precisely

A working diagnosis before any intervention — clinical exam first, then targeted imaging (ultrasound or MRI) only when it will change the plan.

02

Intervene only when necessary

Conservative care first. Procedures are proposed when they are expected to change the outcome — never as a default.

03

Rehabilitate measurably

Structured exercise prescription with measurable return-to-baseline targets — progress you can track, not just feel.

04

For athletes

Football, running, tennis, and court and contact sports. The assessment that matters for sport is not just the injury — it is what the athlete can do, and when they can do it safely again.
